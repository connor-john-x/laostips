Laos offers visa on arrival and e-Visa to many nationalities, making a trip relatively straightforward. But the options differ in cost, eligible entry points and length of stay, so choosing the right one saves time and money.

## The three main options

### 1. Visa on Arrival

The most common choice, issued at designated international entry points.

- **Where**: Wattay International Airport (Vientiane), Luang Prabang International Airport, the First Thai–Lao Friendship Bridge and other major crossings.
- **Stay**: usually 30 days.
- **Cost**: roughly USD 20–40 depending on nationality and port.
- **Materials**: passport valid 6+ months, 1–2 passport photos, onward ticket, accommodation address.

### 2. e-Visa

Apply online via the official site `laoevisa.gov.la`. Good if you want confirmation before you fly and want to skip queues.

- **Processing**: about 3 working days.
- **Stay**: 30 days.
- **Entry points**: only those listed on the official site — check before you travel.

### 3. Embassy visa

Apply in advance at a Lao embassy or consulate. Best for longer stays or multiple entries; single, multiple and business visas are available.

## Which should you choose?

| Your need | Best option |
| --- | --- |
| Spontaneous trip, land/air arrival | Visa on arrival |
| Confirm ahead, avoid queues | e-Visa |
| More than 30 days / multiple entries | Embassy visa |

## Extensions

Visa on arrival and e-Visa can usually be extended inside Laos:

- Apply at the **immigration office** in Vientiane, Luang Prabang and other cities.
- A typical extension is around 30 days and takes a few working days.
- Overstays are fined per day, so extend before your visa expires.

## Practical tips

1. **Passport**: at least 6 months validity and blank pages.
2. **Photos**: bring several; on-site photos cost more.
3. **Cash**: ports accept USD or Lao kip — carry small notes.
4. **Rules change**: always verify with a Lao embassy or the official e-Visa site before departure.
